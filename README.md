# Unstuck

> Turn confusion into better questions.

**Unstuck** is a simple learning support tool designed for students who feel confused during class but struggle to identify what they don't understand or how to ask for help. It helps students capture confusing moments, organize their thoughts, and prepare questions to ask teachers or classmates.

## Features

### 1. Capture Confusion
Quickly record something confusing during class:
- Select a source (`Teacher's Explanation`, `Slides / PPT`, `Reading`, `Class Discussion`, `Activity / Assignment`, `Other`).
- Leave a short note, keyword, or slide number (e.g., `Slide 12 — Schema Theory`).
- Save it to review later.

### 2. Clarify Your Confusion
Use three optional reflection prompts:
- **What do you understand so far?** (or check *"I'm not sure where to start"*)
- **Where are you getting stuck?** (explore 5 categories: `Meaning`, `Logic`, `Connection`, `Application`, `Uncertain`, and select up to 2 items)
- **What kind of help would you like?** (select up to 2 preferences: `Explain`, `Example`, `Connect`, `Guide`, `Check`)

You can answer them in any order or skip any question.

### 3. Prepare Your Question
Try writing your own question first. If you need support, you can:
- **View hints**: See your notes organized by *What I know*, *Where I'm stuck*, and *What I need* (only shows notes you actually provided).
- **See a sample question**: View an example question generated using built-in rule templates without overwriting your own writing.
- **Edit and save**: Write, refine, and save your own question.

### 4. Track Your Progress
Mark each question as:
- `Not Reviewed`
- `Need Help`
- `Partially Resolved`
- `Resolved`

All confusions automatically record their captured timestamp and marked timestamp. You can return to your saved questions anytime.

## How to Use

1. Click **+ Add Confusion** to record a confusing topic during class.
2. Open a saved card to reflect on what you don't understand.
3. Select the types of confusion and help you need.
4. Try writing your own question, using hints or samples if necessary.
5. Ask your teacher or classmates and update the question's status.

---

## How to Run

1. Download or clone the project files.
2. Keep `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` in the same folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser (double-click the file or right-click → Open With Google Chrome / Safari / Edge).

No installation, build process, npm, or account is required.
